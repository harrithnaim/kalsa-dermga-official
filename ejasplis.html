What eJasPlis is

A community delivery marketplace

Malaysians have always asked a friend heading home to tumpang a parcel along the way. eJasPlis turns that everyday kindness into a safe, organised platform — so anyone travelling can help, and anyone sending can find them.

Post

Say what you need

Post a parcel to send, an errand to run, or a trip you are taking — with a photo and the route. It takes under a minute.

Match

Find the right person

eJasPlis matches senders with travellers already going that way. You choose who to go with, and agree the details between you.

Handover

Confirm with a PIN

Pickup and drop-off are confirmed with a simple handover PIN, so both sides know the item changed hands as agreed.

How it works

From post to handover, in four steps

Step one

Post it

A parcel, an errand, or your own trip — with a photo and the route.

Step two

Get matched

See travellers heading your way, with their ratings and past trips.

Step three

Agree & pay direct

Sort the details and any payment directly between the two of you.

Step four

Hand over with a PIN

Confirm pickup and drop-off with a handover PIN. Done.

For senders

Move things, the friendly way

— Reach places couriers make expensive or slow
— See who is carrying your item, and their track record
— Send parcels, documents, or ask for an errand
— Agree the price directly — no hidden fees
For travellers

Turn a trip you’re taking anyway into a good deed

— Help someone out on a journey you were making anyway
— Choose only what fits your route and your bag
— Build a rating and a reputation in the community
— You decide what to carry — you are always in control
Trust & safety

Built for a community, run with care

eJasPlis is a matchmaker that connects people — it does not take custody of your items or your money. That keeps it simple, but it also means both sides should use good judgement, as you would with any community exchange.

Handover PIN

Confirmed, both ways

Pickup and drop-off are each confirmed with a PIN, so there is a clear record that the item changed hands.

Direct payment

Between the two of you

Payment is arranged directly between sender and traveller. eJasPlis never holds your money.

Moderation

Watched over

Listings can be reviewed and removed, and the community is expected to keep it honest, safe and lawful.

Please never send or carry anything illegal, dangerous, or prohibited. Full terms are set out on the eJasPlis platform.
