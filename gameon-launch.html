GameOn Play · Sports for everyone

Just join.

No team? No court? Playing alone? No problem. Find an open game near you, take a spot, pay your share to the host who booked the court, and turn up. You'll leave with a good sweat and new friends.

Our campaign

Sport should be open to everyone. Anyone can play, anyone can connect.

Too many people stop playing because their friends got busy, they moved to a new town, or they don't know anyone with a court booked. GameOn Play puts every open game in one place, so nobody has to sit out.

👤 Playing alone?Join a team that needs one more. Football short a keeper, futsal short a pivot: the spot is yours.
🏸 Open courts, open to anyoneBadminton and pickleball courts that are booked and open for any player. Pick a time, take a spot.
💰 Pay the host, not the whole courtThe player who booked the court hosts the game. You pay only your share, straight to the host.
🤝 New friends every weekSame court, new faces. Play again with the people you liked and build your own crew.
How it works

From your phone to the court in four taps

1Open GameOn PlayIn your browser, no download. Pick your sport.
2Find a gameSee open games by day, place, level and spots left.
3Take a spotPay your share online to the host. Your name is on the list.
4Turn up and playSay hi, play hard, and add your new friends for next week.

For hosts

Booked a court? Fill it, and stop paying for empty spots.

Book a court at one of our partner venues inside GameOn and tick List it on GameOn Play. Once the booking is paid, your game opens by itself. Players join, the cost is split, and each player pays their share to you.

  • Partner courts with free slots shown live
  • Set the sport, level and number of players
  • See who has joined and who has paid
