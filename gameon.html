What you get

The short version

The list updates liveName and phone, that is it. Nobody re-copies a numbered list into the chat forty times.
A real waiting listNumber nineteen waits in order and moves up the moment somebody drops out.
Paying holds your placeSwitch it on and whoever paid is on the list. The organiser stops being out of pocket.
Tap the pitchSee at a glance that nobody has taken left back and four people want to be strikers.
A tidy figure eachTotal divided by whoever actually turned up, rounded to something payable.
Your club has a tableTop scorer, assists, cards and man of the match, filled in by the matches you played.
Before the game

Eighteen places, in the order they asked.

Players tap the link, put their name down, and everybody watching sees the same live list. Tap where you want to play and your name appears there. Pull out before the cutoff and it costs nothing; after it, your place is charged unless somebody on the waiting list takes it — and they are told they are in.

The money

Nobody should be chasing friends for eight ringgit.

It is the single reason organisers give up. Tick the night’s costs — field, bibs, water, referee — and it knows which are per night and which are per head. Balances carry over. When you want to chase, it is one button and one message each, never a public list of names in the group.

After the final whistle

The argument at the mamak has somewhere to go.

Put in the score and who scored, and the same link becomes the voting page. Man of the match, best in defence, paling tak function — those are the defaults, and you can rename or delete any of them. You cannot vote for yourself, only people who played can vote, and a tie is reported as a tie.

Being straight with you

What it will not do

Built out of running Our Sports Club and El Lobo FC, so every awkward corner exists because something went wrong at a real session first.

It will not book your pitchYou still ring the field. It remembers the venue and its usual price so you only type that once.
It will not make people turn upIt measures who actually does, from when they joined rather than the whole season, so a new player is not compared with a founder.
It is not only for footballFutsal, badminton, a run, a cycling group. Positions and a pitch only appear where they make sense.
