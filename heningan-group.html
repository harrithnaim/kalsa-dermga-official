Janda Baik, Pahang

Heningan Campsite

A quiet 3.6-acre site with an acre beside the river, under an hour from Kuala Lumpur. Toilets, showers, running water and parking on site. Bring your own tent, or arrive to one already pitched with beds made up.

Bring your own tent

Riverside lots

Ten lots by the river, each for one family or group, with a parking space. Check in from 2pm, check out by noon.

Nothing to carry

Family glamping tent

A large two-room air tent, pitched under shade before you arrive, with air mattresses, pillows and blankets. For families who want the river without the gear.

Add-ons

Dome tent and comfort kits

Hire a ready-pitched dome for four, or add an air mattress set with pillow and blanket to your own tent.

Groups

Schools, families and teams

Weekday camps for school groups, family days and company teams, including river-ecology sessions with Little Marine Scientists.

Booking and safety

Book and pay the deposit online on BookJe; your booking is confirmed straight away. The river can rise quickly after heavy rain, so we keep the lowest lots closed in bad weather and move your date at no charge if we close them. Swimming is at your own risk and never after heavy rain.
