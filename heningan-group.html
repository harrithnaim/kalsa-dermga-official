Meet the Brands
About

A brand house built around the table

Heningan Group is where Kalsa Dermaga builds and grows its own consumer brands. Our first is Heningan Pizza, and the pipeline behind it is a deliberate one — we would rather launch a small number of things properly than a long list of things adequately. Each brand has to earn its place: made well, priced honestly, and worth recommending to a friend.

Heningan Pizza

Wood-fired pizza made to order — for events, pop-ups and everyday cravings. Dough given the time it needs, toppings kept few and good. Our first brand and the standard the rest are measured against.

More coming to the table

How we build

Gastronomy first

Every Heningan brand starts in the kitchen, not the marketing deck. If the food is not something we would happily eat every week, it does not get a name, a logo, or a launch.
