Who we are

We'd rather run six things properly than announce sixty

Kalsa Dermaga Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian group based in Kuantan, Pahang, with a branch in Shah Alam, Selangor. We start and operate our own businesses rather than invest in other people's.

Every venture here began as a problem someone on our team actually had — a canteen queue full of loose coins, a parcel that needed to get home, a workforce that needed somewhere to recover. We build the smallest honest version, run it ourselves, and only talk about it once it works.

01 Operators, not a holding company. Our own people run the counters, the sessions and the kitchens.
02 Built web-first. Every venture runs on software we built, not spreadsheets and paperwork.
03 Local by default. Pahang and Selangor first — the communities our staff actually live in.
