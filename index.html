Who we are

A Malaysian group from Kuantan

Kalsa Dermaga Sdn Bhd is based in Kuantan, Pahang, with a branch in Shah Alam, Selangor. We start and run our own businesses, and we build the technology behind them.

Each business started with a problem our own team faced, such as a canteen queue full of loose coins, a parcel that needed to get home, or staff who needed somewhere to recover after work. We build a small first version, use it ourselves and improve it before we offer it to anyone else.

01 We run our own businesses. Our own people work the counters, the sessions and the kitchens.
02 We build our own software. Payments, ordering, bookings and HR tools, all made in house.
03 We start local. Our businesses begin in Pahang and Selangor, and our platforms are open to customers anywhere.
