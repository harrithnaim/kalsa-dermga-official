A simple POS for stalls,
cafes and small kitchens

Kalsa POS runs in the browser on a tablet or phone you already have. Take orders, send them to the kitchen, keep track of stock and see the profit on every dish. We use it every day in our own kitchen at Heningan Pizza.

Features

Everything a small kitchen needs

Quick orderingLarge buttons, your own menu and prices, and notes such as no chilli or less sugar.
Kitchen queueOrders move from new to cooking to ready. Unpaid orders stay highlighted until they are settled.
Stock and recipesEach sale deducts the ingredients in its recipe, so you can see what is running low.
Profit per dishSee the food cost and gross profit of every item on your menu.
Cash drawerStart with a float, record cash in and out, then count and close. Staff PINs show who did what, and the till tells you if you are short or over.
Bank checkCompare a day of QR payments with your bank statement and find anything missing.
Customer buzzerCustomers scan a QR code and their phone rings when the order is ready.
e-ReceiptsCustomers keep the receipt on their phone and can request an LHDN e-invoice from it.
Order buzzer

Customers get a buzz when the food is ready

There is no need to buy pagers or call out numbers. The customer scans the QR code on their ticket, and when you tap Ready their phone rings and vibrates. The board also shows which tickets were not scanned, so you know who still needs to be called.

Bank check

Match your sales with your bank statement

Upload your bank statement or paste the lines from your banking app. CSV, Excel and most PDF statements work. Kalsa POS lists any QR payment that is missing or does not match. Your statement is only used for this check and is not saved.

Order tracking

Customers can follow their order

The order page shows received, cooking and ready. If an order is running late, the page says so, which saves your staff from answering the same question again and again.

e-Receipts

Receipts go straight to the customer's phone

After payment, the customer scans a QR code and keeps the receipt. They can save it as a PDF or share it. If they need an e-invoice for a business claim, they request it from the receipt, and you issue it from your own MyInvois account.

Cash, DuitNow QR, card and bank transfer can each be switched on or off in Settings, along with your logo, SSM number, SST details and opening hours.

Good to know

Before you start

A few things worth knowing, so there are no surprises later.

The buzzer needs one extra step on iPhoneApple only allows alerts from a page saved to the home screen. The page shows iPhone users how to do it in two taps. Android phones work straight away.
You need an internet connectionKalsa POS runs online. Wi-Fi or mobile data at the counter is enough.
It is not accounting softwareIt covers sales, costs, margins, cash and the bank check. Your accountant still prepares the full accounts.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to run Kalsa POS?

A tablet, phone or laptop with a modern web browser and an internet connection. Many stalls use a tablet at the counter and a phone in the kitchen.

Which payment methods can I record?

Cash, DuitNow QR, card and bank transfer. You can switch each one on or off in Settings, and switched off methods are hidden at the till.

Does it handle SST and e-invoices?

If you are SST registered, receipts show the SST included in the price. Customers can request an e-invoice from their e-receipt, and you issue it from your own MyInvois account.

Does it work with ORDERje?

Yes. Orders placed at the table go into the same kitchen queue as walk in orders, and your POS menu and photos are used for ORDERje.

Can I use it outside Malaysia?

Yes. Kalsa POS runs in any modern browser, so it works in any country. A few features, such as DuitNow QR and LHDN e-invoice requests, are specific to Malaysia. We set it up with you online.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your business. Tell us what you run and we will send a quotation. You can try it at your own counter before you pay.

Get started

We set it up with you

We load your menu and prices, set up recipes if you want them, and train your staff. In Malaysia we can come to your shop. Anywhere else, we set it up with you online.
