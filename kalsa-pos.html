The counter

Stop shouting numbers at the street.

Restaurants buy plastic pagers that get lost, go home in somebody’s pocket and run flat. Your customer is already holding a better one. They scan, you tap Ready, their phone rings and lights up — and the board tells you which tickets were never scanned, so you know exactly who you still have to call out.

The money

Check the till against your bank.

Every stall owner knows the feeling: the till says one number, the bank says another. Upload a statement or paste the lines out of your banking app — CSV, Excel, most PDFs — and it shows you what does not line up. Your statement is read to answer one question and never stored.

For the customer

They watch the order, not the counter.

Received, cooking, ready. If a ticket is running late it says so — because the question behind “is it ready yet” is almost never the waiting, it is not knowing whether you have been forgotten.

Being straight with you

What it will not do

A stall owner who hears the limits from us trusts the rest of it. One who finds out on a busy Friday does not.

An iPhone will not vibrate for a web pageApple does not allow it. The page walks iPhone customers through the two taps that fix it. Android works straight away.
It will not count your cash drawerIt records how each sale was paid. Counting the notes at the end of the night is still a person with a float.
It is not an accounting packageSales, costs, margins and a bank check. Your accountant still does the rest.
