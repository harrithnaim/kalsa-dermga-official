Land in Bentong, Pahang

Land for lease and partnership

Two plots in Bentong are open to the right tenant or partner: a roadside plot in Benus and a rubber smallholding in Kg Lebu. Short, simple written agreements; offers welcome.

Benus roadside plotKg Lebu rubber land
Benus, Bentong · about 0.4 acre

Roadside plot on the main road

The land

  • About 0.4 acre, beside the main road
  • Agricultural land with direct road access
  • Steady passing traffic, busiest in the durian season

Good for

  • A fruit or durian stall
  • A plant nursery
  • Short-cycle crops by a farmer

Monthly or seasonal lease. Any stall, shade or sign needs our written OK and must follow Majlis Daerah Bentong rules, and the plot is handed back as it was found.

Make an offerWhatsApp

Kg Lebu, Bentong · 6 acres

Rubber smallholding with mature trees

The land

  • 6 acres of rubber, trees over 30 years old
  • Registered with RISDA
  • Due for replanting after a final tapping period

Good for

  • Tappers: tap on a share of factory sales
  • Rubberwood buyers ahead of replanting
  • Replanting and intercrop partners (banana, pineapple and similar)

Tapping is on a share of each sale, agreed in writing. For replanting and intercropping we work with RISDA and talk to partners case by case.

Make an offerWhatsApp

How it works
  1. Tell us what you want to do with the land and your offer
  2. We arrange a site visit
  3. We agree the rent or share in a short written agreement
  4. Rent is invoiced monthly or by season; shares are settled after each sale
