Kalsa Dermaga Presents

Little Marine Scientists (LMS)

Our flagship education initiative introduces children in coastal communities to the marine environment early — splashing, exploring, and discovering along the shoreline in a way that builds curiosity, care, and lifelong love for the sea.

A morning at Pantai Tembeling

On 15 August we ran a Little Marine Scientists session at Pantai Tembeling with 22 children. They spent the morning on the shoreline — looking closely at what the tide had left behind, asking a great many questions, and thoroughly enjoying themselves. Here is how it went.
