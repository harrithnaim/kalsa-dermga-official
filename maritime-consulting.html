Services

What we do

Owners, charterers, agencies

Port call cost check

We review a disbursement account or port call estimate line by line against what it should cost at Kuantan, and show where money is leaking.

New or small agencies

Agency set-up and procedures

Step-by-step procedures for port calls, documents and husbandry, written for your team to follow from the first vessel.

Shippers and forwarders

Vessel and berth planning advice

Scheduling and cargo planning advice to avoid waiting time and surprise costs at Kuantan Port.

Owners without a local person

Representative at Kuantan

Someone on the ground for your port call: attendance, daily reports and problem-solving with the agent, terminal and authorities.

How it works

Start with one port call

  1. A short call: what is hurting and what it costs you
  2. A free first check on one recent port call
  3. A one-page proposal with a fixed price
  4. Half upfront, then the work
  5. Delivery, with the balance invoiced on completion
Fees

Clear prices

Checks, procedures and feasibility notes are quoted as a fixed price before we start. Attendance and representation are charged by the day. Regular clients can move to a monthly retainer.
