🎤 New · Voice ordering

Malas nak scroll?
Just say your order.

“Satu milo ais, dua roti canai, teh tarik kurang manis.”

Tap the mic on the menu and say what you want, in BM or English, the way you'd tell the waiter. ORDERje fills the basket for you. You check it, then pay.

  • Speaks Malaysian. Milo kosong ais, teh o limau, roti telur bawang: it knows the words we actually use.
  • Only what's on the menu. Every item is matched against the outlets' live menu, and it asks you when two outlets sell the same thing.
  • Talk while you browse. Tap to talk any time, even halfway down the menu.
  • You stay in charge. Nothing is ordered until you review the basket and pay.
Try it on a live menu Get it for my food court

Works on phones whose browser supports voice input. Everyone else simply taps to order as usual.

Your phone is the buzzer.
No hardware needed.

Forget the plastic pagers that run flat, go missing and cost money. When the outlet taps Ready, the customer's own phone buzzes and shows a big READY screen, even when it is locked in a pocket.

  • Works with the phone locked. Android straight away; on iPhone, add the page to the home screen once (the page shows how).

Features

Made for food courts and bazaars

One scan for every outletThe QR code on the table opens the menu of the whole food court.
One basket, one paymentSatay from outlet 3 and teh ais from outlet 6 in one payment. Each outlet still receives its own money.
No app to downloadIt opens in the phone’s browser. Customers do not need to sign up.
No pagers to buyThe customer’s own phone rings and vibrates when the food is ready, even when it is locked.
Dine in, takeaway or deliveryCustomers choose how they want their food. Delivery runs through eJasPlis where a rider is available.
Payout report per outletEach outlet gets its own report, checked against what the payment gateway received.
Photos on the menuOutlets can add a photo to each dish. Kalsa POS users get their POS photos on ORDERje automatically.
e-ReceiptsOnce an order is paid, the customer can view and keep the receipt from the order page.
For customers

Customers can sit down and wait

The order page shows received, cooking and ready. Customers can sit down, go back to the car or take a call. When the food is ready, the phone rings, vibrates and shows a full screen alert. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

For outlet owners

A simple board for busy outlets

Outlet owners are busy cooking, so every new order makes a sound. The board fits on a phone screen, and each ticket has one button at a time: start cooking, ready, then handed over. One tap closes the outlet for the night.

For the food court operator

Clear payouts for every outlet

Outlet owners need to trust the payout report. Online payments are checked against what the payment gateway received, and cash taken at a outlet is shown separately, so it is never counted twice. Each outlet sees its own figures.

Good to know

Before you start

A few things worth knowing, so there are no surprises later.

The buzzer needs one extra step on iPhoneApple only allows alerts from a page saved to the home screen. The order page shows iPhone users how to do it in two taps. Android phones work straight away.
Your staff still hand over the foodSomeone still cooks, hands the food over and calls out the few customers who did not scan. The board shows who they are.
Delivery depends on ridersDelivery goes through eJasPlis, our community delivery network, and is only offered where a rider is available.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do customers need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code on the table and the menu opens in their phone browser. They do not need an account.

How do outlet owners receive orders?

On a phone or tablet. Each new order makes a sound, and the outlet taps start cooking, ready and handed over.

How is one payment split between outlets?

The customer pays once for the whole basket. Each outlet sees its own share in its own payout report, and cash taken at a outlet is shown separately.

Can customers pay at the counter?

Yes. Customers can choose to pay online or pay the outlet when they collect their food.

Can we use ORDERje outside Malaysia?

Yes. Ordering and the buzzer work in any country. Online payment currently uses Malaysian payment methods, so outside Malaysia customers pay at the counter. Talk to us about adding your local payment provider.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of outlets and tables. We look at your food court first and then send a quotation.

Get started

Start with one outlet

We usually start with one or two outlets for a week, so the outlet owners can see how it works before the whole food court joins. Tell us where you are. In Malaysia we come and set it up. Outside Malaysia, we do it with you online.
