Features

Made for food courts and bazaars

One scan for every stallThe QR code on the table opens the menu of the whole food court.
One basket, one paymentSatay from stall 3 and teh ais from stall 6 in one payment. Each stall still receives its own money.
No app to downloadIt opens in the phone’s browser. Customers do not need to sign up.
No pagers to buyThe customer’s own phone rings and vibrates when the food is ready, even when it is locked.
Dine in, takeaway or deliveryCustomers choose how they want their food. Delivery runs through eJasPlis where a rider is available.
Payout report per stallEach stall gets its own report, checked against what the payment gateway received.
Photos on the menuStalls can add a photo to each dish. Kalsa POS users get their POS photos on ORDERje automatically.
e-ReceiptsOnce an order is paid, the customer can view and keep the receipt from the order page.
For customers

Customers can sit down and wait

The order page shows received, cooking and ready. Customers can sit down, go back to the car or take a call. When the food is ready, the phone rings, vibrates and shows a full screen alert. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

For stall owners

A simple board for busy stalls

Stall owners are busy cooking, so every new order makes a sound. The board fits on a phone screen, and each ticket has one button at a time: start cooking, ready, then handed over. One tap closes the stall for the night.

For the food court operator

Clear payouts for every stall

Stall owners need to trust the payout report. Online payments are checked against what the payment gateway received, and cash taken at a stall is shown separately, so it is never counted twice. Each stall sees its own figures.

Good to know

Before you start

A few things worth knowing, so there are no surprises later.

The buzzer needs one extra step on iPhoneApple only allows alerts from a page saved to the home screen. The order page shows iPhone users how to do it in two taps. Android phones work straight away.
Your staff still hand over the foodSomeone still cooks, hands the food over and calls out the few customers who did not scan. The board shows who they are.
Delivery depends on ridersDelivery goes through eJasPlis, our community delivery network, and is only offered where a rider is available.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do customers need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code on the table and the menu opens in their phone browser. They do not need an account.

How do stall owners receive orders?

On a phone or tablet. Each new order makes a sound, and the stall taps start cooking, ready and handed over.

How is one payment split between stalls?

The customer pays once for the whole basket. Each stall sees its own share in its own payout report, and cash taken at a stall is shown separately.

Can customers pay at the counter?

Yes. Customers can choose to pay online or pay the stall when they collect their food.

Can we use ORDERje outside Malaysia?

Yes. Ordering and the buzzer work in any country. Online payment currently uses Malaysian payment methods, so outside Malaysia customers pay at the counter. Talk to us about adding your local payment provider.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of stalls and tables. We look at your food court first and then send a quotation.

Get started

Start with one stall

We usually start with one or two stalls for a week, so the stall owners can see how it works before the whole food court joins. Tell us where you are. In Malaysia we come and set it up. Outside Malaysia, we do it with you online.
