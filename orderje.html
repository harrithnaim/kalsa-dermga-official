What you get

The short version

One scan, every stallThe QR on the table opens the whole food court, not one counter.
One basket, one paymentSatay from stall 3, teh ais from stall 6 — and each stall still gets its own money.
No app, no accountIt opens in the browser they already have. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.
No pagers to buyTheir own phone rings, vibrates and fills the screen, locked and in a pocket.
Three ways to eatAt a table, collected on the way past, or delivered through eJasPlis.
The payout adds upOne report per stall, checked against what the gateway actually took.
The buzzer they own

Then they watch it, instead of the counter.

Received, cooking, ready — on the phone that is already in their pocket. They can sit down, go to the car or take a call, and the phone rings, vibrates and fills the screen when the food is up. Nothing to install and no account to create.

For the stall

Three buttons and a noise.

A stall owner does not watch a screen, they cook. So a new order makes a sound, the board is built for a phone propped against a rice cooker, and there is one action per ticket: start cooking, ready, handed over. One tap closes the stall for the night without hiding it.

For whoever runs the place

The money has to add up on Sunday.

Seven stall owners will work out the arithmetic before you do, and the day they stop trusting the payout report is the day the whole thing stops. So cash taken at a counter is reported separately and never counted as ours to pay out, and the day’s takings are compared with what the payment gateway says it actually took.

Being straight with you

What it will not do

Every one of these is on this page on purpose.

An iPhone will not vibrate for a web pageApple does not allow it. On iPhone the alert arrives once the customer adds the page to their home screen — two taps, and the page walks them through it.
It will not replace your staffSomebody still cooks, hands the food over, and calls out the order of anybody who did not scan. The board says plainly which ones those are.
It is not a delivery companyDelivery goes through eJasPlis, our own community network, and only where a rider is actually available.
