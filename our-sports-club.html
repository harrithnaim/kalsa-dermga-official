Kalsa Dermaga Presents

Our Sports Club

A social club that happens to play sport. The game brings people together, and the people keep them coming back. Open to our team, their families, and anyone in the community who would rather meet over a match than over a meeting. well, ever heard networking through sports?

Programme

What a week looks like

Weekly

Futsal Nights

Mixed teams drawn on the night, so you play with different people each week. All levels are welcome.

Weekly

Badminton Socials

Doubles rotation, everyone gets a court, nobody sits out for long. The easiest session to bring a first-timer to.

Weekend

Run & Walk Group

A steady loop at a pace that lets you hold a conversation. Walkers are welcome too, and make up half the group.

Always

Makan After

We always eat together after every session. It is where most of the friendships in the club begin.

Monthly

Members' Mixer

An open evening for members to introduce what they do and what they are looking for. It is a good way to meet people who could help your work.

Seasonal

Family Open Days

Sessions built so members can bring partners, parents and children along, with smaller games, longer breaks and more food.

Club Kit

Wear the badge

Our club jersey is available to anyone who plays with us, and to anyone who simply wants one. It is the same shirt our regulars wear on futsal nights.

Buying one is the easiest way to join in. Tell us your size and we will let you know the price, the colours currently available, and when the next batch is ready.
