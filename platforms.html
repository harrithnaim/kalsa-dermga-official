Newest

Bookings and appointments

BookJe is for businesses that rent out a space by the hour or the night. KDserve is for businesses where customers book a person, such as a therapist or a barber.

BookJe

One booking system for courts, halls, chalets and boats

The venue sets its own prices, peak hours and deposit. Customers pick a free slot, see the price and pay straight away, and a slot that has been taken cannot be booked again. The owner sees every booking on a calendar, can take phone bookings, block out time for maintenance and set up weekly regulars.

It is in use today at a futsal field, an 800 guest wedding hall, a chalet resort and a boat charter, and it has a counter for selling drinks, bibs and rentals.

The customer side

Customers book and pay online

Each venue gets its own booking link and QR code. Customers can see which slots are taken, the price of each slot, and can book several hours together. They do not need an app or an account.

KDserve

Customers book the person they want

Each staff member has their own calendar and only the services they can perform. Every sale records who did the work, so commission at the end of the month is worked out for you. It also handles packages, members, expenses and the cash drawer.

We built it for El Medina Spa, and it now runs in a barbershop too.

The customer side

Online booking with prices up front

Customers book from a link or a QR code at the door. Each treatment shows its price and duration before they choose. The next visit can be booked before the customer leaves.
