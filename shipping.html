Portcalls

One place for the whole port call

Portcalls is our own system. It replaces the email-and-phone scramble of vessel husbandry with one secure, trackable workflow, used by our team at Kuantan Port every day.

BookIntent to call, in minutesVessel details, ETA, cargo and the services you need, in one form. No account needed for the first request.
EstimateInstant port cost estimateEnter gross tonnage, length, hours alongside and cargo to see port dues, pilotage, tugs, mooring and wharfage from Kuantan Port's published tariff.
DocumentsUpload once, kept privateManifests, crew lists, IDs and certificates are never on a public link. Only you and our operations team can open them.
TrackLive status timelineRequest received, under review, berth assigned, pilot requested, customs cleared, services in progress, completed: every change is logged with the time.
NotifyAn email on every changeYou don't need to call for an update. Each status change emails you, and our team is alerted the moment a request lands.
CostProforma DA you can checkYour proforma disbursement account is built line by line from the current tariff, then marked sent and funds received, so nothing is a surprise.
How it works

From request to sail-away in four steps

You see every step on your dashboard. Behind it, our team does the work at the port.

  1. SubmitSend the vessel details, ETA and cargo, and upload the manifest and crew list.
  2. We are alertedOur operations team is notified at once, reviews the call and assigns a handler.
  3. We coordinateBerth, pilot, port authority and customs, handled for you at Kuantan Port.
  4. You trackYour dashboard updates as each milestone clears, with an email every time.

Landbridge

Kuantan Port to Port Klang, on one bill

East coast to west coast without the long sea route around the peninsula. Cargo for the west coast can land at Kuantan Port and cross the peninsula by land to Port Klang, or the other way, with Kalsa Dermaga as the one party you deal with and one bill for the whole move.

What we handle

  • Port call and agency at Kuantan
  • Container moves between the ports
  • One quote, one bill

Built for the ECRL

  • Ready for the ECRL rail link's cargo services
  • Our Landbridge Ops system reads shipping line messages, allocates containers and bills each move
