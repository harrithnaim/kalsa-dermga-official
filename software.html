How these got built

Nothing here was designed in a meeting

We are an operator before we are a software company. We run a pizza kitchen, a sports club, a spa and a school payment system, and every awkward corner of this software exists because something went wrong at a real counter first.

That is also why none of these pages quote a price. What a seven-stall food court needs is not what one bakery needs, so we would rather come and look at the place than send you a number.

01 We use it first. Kalsa POS runs Heningan Pizza. GameOn runs Our Sports Club and El Lobo FC.
02 We set it up with you. Your menu, your prices, your squad — we would rather spend an afternoon at your counter than email you a login.
03 We tell you what it will not do. Every page has a short honest section about the limits, on purpose.
