How it works

From your catalogue to the ship

You make and pack the goods. We handle the buyer, the paperwork and the port.

  1. You send your catalogue and price list

    Prices ex-works or FOB Kuantan Port, the minimum order, monthly capacity, packing details, shelf life and certifications.

  2. We check the product for Indonesia

    We confirm with our partners that the product is wanted and can be imported, including any registration or certification it needs there.

  3. We visit and agree terms

    We visit your factory, agree the price, quantity, packing and delivery date, and confirm everything in writing.

  4. Purchase order and payment terms

    Each order comes with a written purchase order. Production starts once the agreed deposit or letter of credit is in place.

  5. We prepare the export

    Customs declaration, ASEAN Certificate of Origin (Form D), and any health or halal certificates the product needs.

  6. Shipment from Kuantan Port

    We book the cargo, arrange the port side at Kuantan and track the shipment to Batam, Tanjung Pinang or Natuna.

Questions

Questions from suppliers

Do you charge suppliers to send a catalogue?

No. Sending us your catalogue and price list costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Will you share our price list with other companies?

No. We present products to our partners under Kalsa Dermaga's name and do not publish or pass your price list to other suppliers.

We have never exported. Can we still take part?

Yes. We prepare the export documents, including the customs declaration and the ASEAN Certificate of Origin (Form D), and arrange the shipment through Kuantan Port. You prepare the goods and the packing list.

Which prices should we quote?

Ex-works from your factory, or FOB Kuantan Port if you deliver to the port. Please say which, and include the minimum order quantity.

Does Indonesia accept every product?

No. Some products need Indonesian registration or certification before import, for example SNI for tyres, batteries and some steel and building materials, and BPOM registration and halal certification for many foods. We check each product with our partners before any order.

When do we get paid?

Payment terms are agreed in writing in each purchase order before production starts, with a deposit or a letter of credit in place.

Which port do the goods ship from?

Kuantan Port, Pahang, to Batam, Tanjung Pinang and Natuna in Indonesia's Riau Islands.
